# 11\. Typography and Interface Style

- Use a modern geometric sans-serif font such as Outfit, Manrope, Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans.
- Use bold, condensed-feeling headings with short industrial messages.
- Keep body text easy to scan with comfortable spacing and a maximum readable width.
- Use rounded but not overly soft cards; 8-14 px corner radius is suitable.
- Use subtle borders and shadows. Avoid excessive glassmorphism, neon colours or decorative effects that weaken industrial credibility.
- Use orange for primary actions and blue for secondary actions or links.
- Animations should be refined: reveal-on-scroll, counters, card hover, image zoom and smooth sliders. Avoid heavy or distracting motion.
