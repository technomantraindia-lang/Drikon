Our Origin Story

The Genesis of DRIKON: A Strategic Merger

DRIKON Infratech Pvt. Ltd. was established through the strategic merger of two industry-leading enterprises: **Patel Engineers** and **Vardayini Infratech**.

Prior to the merger, **Patel Engineers** operated as a premier structural engineering consultancy, managing complex high-load design calculations, drafting, and planning. Simultaneously, **Vardayini Infratech** served as a high-capacity steel fabrication facility, manufacturing roofing sheets, roll-formed purlins, and structural columns.

Recognizing the rising demand for a single-source turnkey contractor, the two organizations consolidated. This merger combined engineering design accuracy with raw production capacity under a single, unified leadership.

Our Methodology

How We Work

We streamline coordination, design engineering, fabrication, and execution to minimize project downtime and ensure maximum structural integrity.

1. Consult & Design (📐)

We collaborate with your project planners to analyze site criteria, compute precise dead/live load requirements, and draft 3D structural blueprints aligned with national safety codes.

2. Manufacture & QA (⚙️)

Components are fabricated in our high-capacity facility using automated purlin and sheet roll-formers. Every rafter and column undergoes strict thickness and weld checks.

3. Deliver & Erect (🏗️)

Ready-to-assemble members are transported safely to the site. Our technical erection crews execute the assembly using strict safety gear and crane alignment checks.
